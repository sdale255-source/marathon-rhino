Everything you need between now and race day

Simple enough to use every day, detailed enough to keep you honest about your training.

GPS run tracking

Record runs live with a timer, distance and pace — plus a projected marathon finish time based on how you're running today.

Race-day countdown

Set your next race and goal time. Marathon Rhino counts down the days and shows the pace you need to hit your goal.

Training calendar

Log runs and rest days, see this week at a glance, and look back month by month at the miles (or kilometres) you've put in.

Maps of your races

Every race you've finished is pinned on maps of the US, Europe, North and South America, and a spinning globe.

Achievements

Unlock milestones automatically as you train — long runs, big weeks and big months, in miles or kilometres.

Race history & photos

Keep a record of every race — times, places and photos — and read marathon news and articles inside the app.

Save the Rhino International

Supports field programmes that protect rhinos from poaching and help populations recover across Africa and Asia.

World Land Trust

Buys and protects threatened habitat — rainforests, grasslands and wetlands — so wildlife has somewhere to live.

"What we receive" means subscription revenue the company actually receives after App Store or payment-processing fees, refunds and sales taxes. Donations are made by The Marathon Rhino Company LLC from its own revenue; they are not donations by subscribers and are not tax-deductible for subscribers.

About us

Marathon Rhino is made by The Marathon Rhino Company LLC, a small, independent company based in Pennsylvania. Our goal is simple: make marathon training a little more fun, and make every mile count for something bigger than a finish time.

We don't show ads, we don't sell your data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. Read our Privacy Policy for the details.
